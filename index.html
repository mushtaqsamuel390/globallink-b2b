A PLATFORM INITIATIVE BY HEAVENLY HAND MINISTRY

Built around access, transparency and accountability.

GlobalLink B2B is a free-access international business networking initiative. It aims to help buyers and suppliers discover one another and begin business conversations across borders. The platform is an initiative of Heavenly Hand Ministry, Pakistan. The Ministry does not guarantee transactions or automatically verify businesses.
